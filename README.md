## About me

I'm a **full-stack software engineer** who builds enterprise applications, backend platforms and modern SaaS products — combining **C# / .NET** with **React, Next.js and TypeScript**.

As the **founding engineer at Jaxsuite AI**, I built a SaaS product alone from zero to MVP and then grew it into a five-person team. I've worked across the full lifecycle: architecture, database design, APIs, frontend, third-party integrations, deployment and production hardening.

I care about software that is **maintainable, secure, scalable and reliable** — not just software that works.

**How I work**

- Scale systems without adding needless complexity.
- Keep architectures clean, modular and easy to reason about.
- Treat security, performance and observability as fundamentals, not extras.
- Start from the business problem, then pick the technology.
